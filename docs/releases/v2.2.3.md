# Deckhand 2.2.3 — try-on, section by section

Try-on was run on a test site with one section of every common kind:
- a notice bar, service cards, a lone card, a shadcn card;
- blog cards, product cards, stats, a logo row, a team, steps;
- a contact form, a newsletter, a login, badges, integrations;
- a comparison table, a big quote and a button.

Every variant was screenshotted and checked for words that are not yours, words of yours that went missing, and
broken layout. Everything found is fixed and pinned by a test.

**Sections that now work**
- **Logo clouds.** Before, nothing was offered. Now your own logos go into the design's logo row. The design's
  demo brands never show as yours.
- **Stats.** 120+, 98% and 24h go into the design's figures. Before, one could become the section title.
- **Notice bars.** Your sentence is kept and becomes the headline. Before, only the link survived.
- **Teams.** Names and roles stay in their places, and each photo carries your name.
- **Comparison tables.** Your table fills a design's table row by row, with your column names. Plan-card designs
  that scrambled it are no longer offered.

**Your forms keep working.** On a contact or login section, your own form (its fields, where it sends, its
button) takes the design form's place. A design with no place for your form (a newsletter's email field) is not
offered.

**Product cards.** A design's "Popular" badge no longer takes your product name, which used to push every word
one slot down. "Get Started" becomes your "Add to cart", and a design's "/month" never sits next to a product
price.

**Cards and buttons.** Designs that write their own words into props or switch labels on hover now show yours.
A card or a button must keep all of your words to be offered. Tweet embeds, hover cards and a vendor's branded
button are no longer offered as cards or buttons.

**Keep builds.** Some designs call your own components with options yours do not have (a card "outline" style).
That worked in the preview but broke `next build` once kept. Those options are now fitted to your components,
and a real production build of a site with the new sections kept passes.

**Honest by default.** Any word a design still shows of its own is dashed, even inside a button or in a demo
music player. Keep removes the marks. When no design fits, the message says why in plain words ("none has room
for your content — the closest keeps 7 of your 16 pieces") and offers the AI draft.

**Phones.** On a narrow screen the variant bar used to run off the edge (no way to reach Discard). It now wraps,
with the design's name on top.

Upgrade: re-run `install.sh` / `install.ps1`, then restart `tryon serve`.
