# Deckhand 2.2.2 — try-on, Tune and Site, audited live

Try-on, Tune and Site were run knob by knob in a real browser on three apps, each built the way owners build them:
a default Next app with shadcn, a pnpm app with a `src/` folder, and a Vite app. Everything found is fixed and
pinned by a test.

**Your words, in the right places — and nothing that is not yours.**
- FAQs keep every question and answer. Before, the design's own questions could show as if they were yours.
- Testimonials fit: quote to quote, name to name. Company logos inside the cards are hidden one by one.
- Pricing handles "Custom" and "/month".
- Footers keep your Contact / Company columns with their links. A site without a Deckhand plan no longer shows the
  design's demo menus or its GitHub links.
- Any design text left in a list is dashed and listed as demo copy.

**Navbars.** 45 navbar designs now, from every Tailark hero's own header. Each one carried your whole navbar in
the test.

**Vite works.** Designs written for Next render in Vite through small local stand-ins, and the page-still-builds
check now works on Vite too (in about 2.5 s instead of timing out).

**Tune.**
- Clicking a button tunes the button.
- "Pill" or "raised" on a button adds the look when the button had none.
- A knob with nothing to change tells you where to go instead.
- Reset keeps your own edits if you changed the file meanwhile.

**Site.** Undo goes back one apply at a time, all the way to the original. The preview was checked to match the
applied site pixel for pixel, fonts and dark mode included.

Upgrade: re-run `install.sh` / `install.ps1`, then restart `tryon serve`.
