# v2.0.0 — Deckhand v2: one skill from idea to a live business you own

**The five v0 skills are now one (`skills/deckhand`), run by one command line (`dh`) that gives the agent exactly
one next step and proves every "done" with a script.**

- **Phases with checkpoints.** define → research → plan → build → brand → try-on → review → deploy → operate. In
  phased mode (the default) the agent stops at 4 checkpoints until you say go, and a phase is only done when its
  check passes.
- **Try-on that works.** Click any section of your running site: licensed designs appear in your colours, with
  your own words, links and photos. ← → compares them, and Keep or Discard is exact. No AI call per click.
- **Tune and Site.** Adjust a section (quieter, bolder, airier…) or the whole look (accent, greys, corners,
  fonts) instead of replacing it. Every change is exact and can be undone. Or just say it in chat.
- **Found on Google and in AI answers.** Crawl rules, a sitemap, titles, canonicals, share cards, your business
  details, a real 404 page and instant Bing indexing, all written from your own facts. Launch-breakers block the
  release. What only you can do (Google Business Profile, Search Console, Bing, reviews) stays on your to-do list
  until it's done.
- **Continue from any session, with any AI.** A one-page summary of the project is rebuilt after every step.
  `dh resume` tells you whether it's safe to start fresh, and why not if it isn't. Claude, Codex, Cursor or any
  other agent carries on from the exact next step. Claude Code can load it on its own: `./install.sh --claude-hook`.
- **Client work stays separate.** `dh init --for client` keeps that client's settings and keys in its own
  project, on your computer only.
- **It learns from its mistakes, without guessing.** `dh autopsy` turns a session log into proven fixes, and the
  next session gets them before the error happens.
- **Nothing joins on trust, and your work becomes your library.** Templates and component libraries are vetted
  (licence, paywalls, committed secrets, dead repos). A site you shipped becomes a private starter on your own
  GitHub (`dh harvest --push`).

**Proof you can rerun:** CI on Ubuntu and Windows runs 46 try-on + 68 control-plane + 40 server-client + 17 repo
tests, all offline. Not yet proven live: a full `dh deploy ship` against a real server, the first real run of the
$0 Oracle track, and the Claude Code hook inside a live Claude Code session.

**Upgrading from v0.x:** run `./install.sh` again (Windows: `install.ps1`). Tokens stored by v0's ops keyring
(`~/.vps-ops`) are still read. Everything that changed: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/takimdigital/deckhand-skill/blob/v2.0.0/CHANGELOG.md)
· step by step: [use cases](https://github.com/takimdigital/deckhand-skill/blob/v2.0.0/docs/USE-CASES.md).
